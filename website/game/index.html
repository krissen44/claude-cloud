How a fight works

Bite, guard, taunt

Ten rounds, healthiest at the bell wins. Traits from the NFT metadata become abilities that cost energy. The opponent reads your habits — a fixed rhythm gets punished.

4–7 damage. Beats a taunt for extra damage.

🛡️ Guard

🐶 Taunt

+2 energy and chips 2 through a guard. Eats extra from a bite.
